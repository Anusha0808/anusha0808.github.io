# my journey
I love solving problems! I love the journey of coming up with a solution and how it challenges your brain to think in new and different ways each time.This is one of the reasons why I decided to pursue research. My passion for it all started when I started competitive programming in my last year of high school. 

It taught me how to internalise the problem and, if I am able to come up with a solution for it, how to internalise that as well. How to convert the mathematical conditions that I am getting into intuition. How can I "spot" this kind of solution if this problem comes up in the future.

I wrote ZCO and INOI in 2023, which are the first and second rounds of the informatics olympiad in India. As a result, I got selected for the national training camp, among with 30 other students throughout the country. I also got a chance to be in India's team for [[European Girls Olympiad In Informatics (EGOI)]] in 2023. It was in Lund, Sweden at that time and I got to have a lot of interesting interactions with people (and alot of food!!). I remember discussing with the professors I met there and asking them if I should pursue research or not. Their talks and presentations, and the fun they had while delivering those talks, made me certain to pursue research. Shortly after that, I joined Chennai Mathematical Institute, which is the leading theoretical computer science research in India, and I have never regretted that decision in my life.
