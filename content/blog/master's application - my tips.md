## why I chose MPRI
As I stated in the above post, I was looking for masters programmes which are good in logic, games on graphs and algorithms. The last topic is mostly considered disjoint from the first, therefore I was having a hard time finding programmes which had a variety of courses across these disciplies, and that the material in each course was also intensive enough. I found MPRI was meeting my requirements and that the course was taught in English.

# general points to keep in mind while finding a uni for you

### breadth or depth?
First, ask yourself, do you want breadth or depth? Do you want a wide variety of courses from different areas, or do you wanna specialise in some particular field?

### professors?
Second, know the professors of the place you are considering! Take the masters programme you are looking at, find the professors who **regularly** teach courses in it, check out their profile, their research, and if they have notes for the courses they teach. Basically, try to answer the question - do they enjoy teaching and actively try to make the course interesting? 

This is because, not all professors good at research really like to teach. A place can have one of the best research labs but not be good at their teaching programme. Know and spot the difference

### talk to people!!
Mail students whom you know are in the course, if you have some common link , or if you don't, that is okay too. But just mail them! Mail professors if you are interested in their masters programme, ask them stupid questions. But engage and talk to people about it. You might only get little pieces of information from each of them, but little drops make up an ocean :)
