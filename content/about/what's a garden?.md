## this garden
*"A throw of seeds."* 
> *Some grow into beautiful bushes with flowers, yielding fruit that others can enjoy. Some are left unnurtured.* 

This is my little corner of the internet where I keep things I am learning, thinking about, reading, or just find interesting. Some things here will be polished. Some will be half-baked. There will probably be some flowers and beautiful trees, and there will probably also be quite a few dormant seeds. Feel free to wander around :)
