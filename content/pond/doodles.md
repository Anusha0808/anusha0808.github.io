I like to doodle and randomly draw shapes and figures, or fill a colouring book. I have tried sketching, but I realised that I enjoy seeing colours. I enjoy when things are bright and colorful, which is why I LOVE acrylic [[Paintings|painting]]!  On this page, I have used acrylic paint markers. I love them! They are vibrant, saturated and come in so many colours. I can even blend two different colours to some extent, and I do not have to pick up my acrylic tubes to satisfy my craving. (I mean, who likes to clean after painting :/)

I have used markers from BRUSTO, it was a begginer 10-12 marker set. I am soon going to buy a bigger set with alot more colours and dual tips :D
