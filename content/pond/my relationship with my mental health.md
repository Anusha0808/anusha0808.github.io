They say not all days are the same, but sometimes some days can look exactly like the ones before it - frozen and dull.
So I have found some other inspiring sentence to be my soothing balm - *Life has different seasons. This too is a season, it shall pass;*
I have been inspired by Swedish people. Their lives are hard, and they have to prepare every year for the dark months that follow. But they know for sure its just a season. That the sun is gonna come up, and it is gonna be alright. Till then, let us sit with the darkness, maybe talk to it a bit, and learn to co exist with it.
