I love barks!! (of trees :P)
I find their patterns and textures to be very interesting and unique. It sets apart a tree from the ones around it, just by the way its contours are laid out. Maybe it tells its experience? Maybe it tells its growth? Maybe it is a way they tell us about themselves. 
Whatever it is, it makes me curious and it makes me wonder 💭
